A **traditional learning path**: a video course with hands-on labs.
Learners watch narrated videos (4-6 minutes each, split into 2-4 chapters
the editor cuts between screencast footage in Premiere), read the companion
article that ships with every video, and practice in a closing lab at the
end of each module. The rules and the research behind them are in
`.claude/house-style.md` "Traditional course design"; linux-intermediate
(finished and posted) is the worked example of a whole path, and its
outline is `linux-intermediate-path.md` at the repo root.
